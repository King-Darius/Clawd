# Public sources

Clawd's House is an independent fan project. It does not redistribute source
animation frames. Its code-rendered motion uses observations from public mascot
media and original clean-room drawing.

## Animation and interaction references

- [Official Claude Code introduction](https://youtu.be/fl1DSmwQKKY) — coffee
  timing, head tilt, cup orientation and held drinking pose.
- [Codrops mascot animation study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/05/05/reverse-engineering-claude-ais-mascot-animations-with-svg-and-gsap/) — construction, walking, jumping, flag and celebration studies.
- [Clawd on Desk](https://github.com/rullerzhou-afk/clawd-on-desk) — community
  examples of compact desktop actions.
- [clawd-emotes-skill](https://github.com/xixicc186/clawd-emotes-skill) — community
  emote references, including painting and seasonal scenes.
- [Mantella](https://github.com/art-from-the-machine/Mantella) and
  [Pantella](https://github.com/Pathos14489/Pantella) — general inspiration for
  persistent NPC context and bounded world actions. Their code and game
  integrations are not incorporated.

## Mind and connectome references

- [Janelia MaleCNS v1.0 data](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) - CC-BY-4.0 anatomical source for the optional adult-core prototype. Graph data is not bundled. The configured desktop bridge can activate this core; portable mode defaults to the larval graph. Its conductance constants, sensory population drives and outcome-based learning are engineered mechanisms, not a validated biological reconstruction.

- [Winding et al., *The connectome of an insect brain*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7614541/) — published larval *Drosophila* connectivity used as the anatomical starting graph.
- [Closed-form continuous-time neural networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-022-00556-7) and the [Liquid Time-constant Networks paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04439) — background for continuous-time adaptive dynamics.
- [Liquid AI research overview](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/liquid-neural-networks-research) — accessible background reading on liquid neural networks.
- [NVIDIA, *Nemotron 3 Nano Omni: Efficient and Open Multimodal Intelligence*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24954) — reference for modality-specific encoders, learned projectors into a shared language sequence, staged multimodal alignment and quality/preference feedback. Clawd uses only a small runtime-inspired observation interface, not the paper's pretrained model or training recipe.

The simulation adds engineered signs, time constants, house inputs, concept
assemblies, readouts and plasticity. It does not claim to reproduce a biological
brain or the cited learned models.

## Books and music

Public-domain book editions are derived from sources such as
[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) and other clearly attributed
public archives. Catalogue entries identify the edition or translation where
known. Built-in sea-shanty melodies are new house transcriptions of traditional
melodies rather than redistributed MIDI arrangements.
The three `vision-cards` pictures are original pixel studies drawn for this
simulation: a lighthouse and boat, a bridge and lantern, and coffee by a
window. They contain no imported photographs, animation frames or metadata.
The [Project Gutenberg children's literature shelf](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/bookshelf/20)
was used as a broad editorial comparator for Clawd's original stories; no
source prose was copied into those stories.
